The Framework Convention on AI (Vilnius Convention, CETS 225) is the first binding
international treaty on AI, adopted by the Council of Europe in 2024 and open for
signature by both Council of Europe members and non-member states. It applies a
risk-based approach aligned with the EU AI Act and requires parties to adopt
legislative or other measures to ensure AI systems respect human rights.
