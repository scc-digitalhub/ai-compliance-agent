The European Banking Authority (EBA) and European Central Bank (ECB) have issued
supervisory expectations on the use of machine learning in credit risk modelling,
covering model validation, explainability, bias, and governance. These complement
the EU AI Act for AI systems in the financial sector (EU AI Act Annex III covers
creditworthiness AI as high-risk).

# Key Requirements

| Area | Requirement |
|------|-------------|
| Model validation | Independent validation of ML model performance and stability |
| Explainability | Local and global explanations for credit decisions |
| Bias and fairness | Demographic bias testing in credit scoring |
| Model risk management | Inventory, tiering, and ongoing monitoring of ML models |
| Documentation | Model documentation including data lineage and assumptions |
| Human oversight | Human review of outlier decisions and model-in-use monitoring |
