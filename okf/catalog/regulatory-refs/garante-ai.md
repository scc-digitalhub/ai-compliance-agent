The Italian Garante per la protezione dei dati personali (Garante) has issued
binding enforcement decisions and non-binding guidance on AI systems processing
personal data. The 2023 ChatGPT decision established concrete requirements for
GPAI systems operating in Italy, including age verification, user rights, and
training data transparency. These requirements exceed baseline GDPR obligations.

# Key Enforcement Points

| Topic | Requirement |
|-------|-------------|
| Age verification | GPAI services must implement measures to exclude minors |
| Training data basis | Lawful basis must be identified for training data processing |
| Data subject rights | Rights under GDPR Arts. 15–22 must be fully honoured |
| Transparency | Users must be informed that content is AI-generated |
| Data quality | Factually incorrect personal data must be rectifiable or suppressed |
| DPIA | High-risk AI profiling requires DPIA with Garante consultation |
