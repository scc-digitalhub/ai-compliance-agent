# Sensitive and Protected Attributes

Concrete [AttributeRef](/context/AttributeRef.md) instances for attributes
subject to special legal protection or fairness obligations. Use these as
candidates in `protected_attributes` in a [ComplianceContext](/context/ComplianceContext.md)
and in `attributes` in a [DataComplianceSpec](/specs/DataComplianceSpec.md).
