# Full Requirement Text

Personal data shall be processed lawfully, fairly and in a transparent manner (lawfulness, fairness, transparency); collected for specified, explicit and legitimate purposes and not further processed in incompatible ways (purpose limitation); adequate, relevant and limited to what is necessary (data minimisation); accurate and kept up to date (accuracy); kept in a form permitting identification no longer than necessary (storage limitation); and processed with appropriate security (integrity and confidentiality).
