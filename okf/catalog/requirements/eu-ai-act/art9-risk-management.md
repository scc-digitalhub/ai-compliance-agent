# Full Requirement Text

Providers of high-risk AI systems shall establish, implement, document and maintain a risk management system, identifying and analysing known and reasonably foreseeable risks, estimating and evaluating risks that may emerge from the intended purpose and reasonably foreseeable misuse, adopting suitable risk management measures, and testing the AI system to verify risk mitigations are effective.
