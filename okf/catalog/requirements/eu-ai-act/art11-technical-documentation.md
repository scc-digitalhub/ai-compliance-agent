# Full Requirement Text

Before placing a high-risk AI system on the market or putting it into service, providers shall draw up technical documentation containing at minimum the information required by Annex IV, in a manner to demonstrate compliance with the requirements of Chapter III Section 2 and provide national competent authorities and notified bodies with the necessary information to assess compliance.
