# Full Requirement Text

The organisation shall establish and implement controls to ensure that AI systems support appropriate human oversight. This includes designing systems to allow humans to: understand AI system behaviour and outputs; intervene in AI system operation; override AI system decisions where necessary; and monitor AI system performance and detect anomalies. The level of human oversight shall be commensurate with the risk level of the AI system.
