# Full Requirement Text

The organisation shall maintain documentation that enables appropriate transparency of AI systems to relevant stakeholders. Documentation shall include: description of the AI system's intended purpose and scope; summary of the AI system's capabilities and limitations; description of the data used for development and testing; information about the AI system's decision-making process appropriate to the stakeholder; and information enabling users to correctly interpret AI system outputs.
