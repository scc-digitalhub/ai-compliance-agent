# Full Requirement Text

The organisation shall establish processes for acquiring data for AI systems that ensure: data is fit for its intended purpose; appropriate rights and permissions exist for the intended use; data quality requirements are defined and verified; data provenance and lineage are documented; and privacy and data protection requirements are met throughout the data lifecycle.
