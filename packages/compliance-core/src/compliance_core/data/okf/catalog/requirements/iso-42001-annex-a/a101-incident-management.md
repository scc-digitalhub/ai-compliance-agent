# Full Requirement Text

The organisation shall establish and maintain processes for managing incidents and errors related to AI systems. These shall include: mechanisms for detecting and reporting AI incidents and errors; processes for investigating root causes; corrective and preventive actions; communication of incidents to relevant stakeholders; and learning from incidents to improve AI system performance and governance.
