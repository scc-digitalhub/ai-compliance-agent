# Full Requirement Text

Providers of very large online platforms and very large online search engines shall put in place reasonable, proportionate and effective mitigation measures, tailored to the specific systemic risks identified pursuant to Article 34. Such measures may include adapting the design, features or functioning of the service, including recommender systems; adapting content moderation processes; strengthening internal processes or supervision; initiating or adjusting cooperation with trusted flaggers; taking awareness measures.
