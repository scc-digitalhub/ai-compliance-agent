# Full Requirement Text

AI actors should assess and address the potential harms of AI systems throughout their lifecycle and ensure that the use of AI is proportionate to the aims sought. If trade-offs between principles must be made, particular attention should be paid to the potential harm that may result from such trade-offs and to taking steps to avoid or minimise such harm.
