ISO/IEC 23894 provides detailed guidance on implementing risk management for AI
systems, complementing ISO/IEC 42001 and aligning with ISO 31000 (general risk
management). It covers risk identification, analysis, evaluation, treatment, and
monitoring specific to AI systems.
