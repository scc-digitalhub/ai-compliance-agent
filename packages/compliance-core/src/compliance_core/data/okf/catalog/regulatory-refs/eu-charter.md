The EU Charter is legally binding on EU institutions and member states when
implementing EU law. It provides the constitutional foundation for GDPR (Arts. 7–8)
and the EU AI Act's prohibition on discriminatory AI (Art. 21). AI systems deployed
in the EU must not violate Charter rights.
