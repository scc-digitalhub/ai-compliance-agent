Adopted unanimously by all 193 UNESCO member states in November 2021, this
Recommendation provides a global normative framework for AI ethics. Although
non-binding, it is the broadest international consensus on AI ethics principles
and informs national AI strategies and regulations worldwide.

# Eleven Core Values and Principles

| Principle | Description |
|-----------|-------------|
| Proportionality and Do No Harm | AI should not exceed what is necessary for the legitimate aim |
| Safety and Security | AI systems must be technically robust and secure |
| Fairness and Non-discrimination | AI must not create or reinforce unfair bias |
| Sustainability | AI must be environmentally and socially sustainable |
| Right to Privacy and Data Protection | Personal data must be protected throughout the AI lifecycle |
| Human Oversight and Determination | Humans retain the right to override AI decisions |
| Transparency and Explainability | AI processes and decisions must be understandable |
| Responsibility and Accountability | Clear accountability for AI outcomes |
| Awareness and Literacy | Stakeholders must be equipped to engage critically with AI |
| Multi-stakeholder Governance | AI governance must be inclusive and participatory |
| Inclusiveness and Access | AI benefits must be accessible to all |
